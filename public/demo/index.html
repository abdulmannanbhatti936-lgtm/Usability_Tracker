Our Story

This long section tests scroll depth tracking. See how far users actually read.

NovaBuy was founded in 2019 with a simple mission: make premium technology accessible to everyone. We started in a small garage in San Francisco, bootstrapped with just $5,000 and a dream.

Our first product was a simple wireless mouse that sold 200 units in the first month. The community response was overwhelming — users loved the build quality and the honest pricing. That feedback became our north star.

By 2021, we had expanded to 50 products and crossed $1M in annual revenue. More importantly, we maintained a 4.9-star average rating across 15,000+ verified reviews. The secret? We listen obsessively to our customers.

In 2023, we launched the NovaBuy Pro membership program, giving members early access to new releases, exclusive discounts, and priority customer support. Within six months, 40% of our customers became members.

Today we ship to 190+ countries and have helped over 100,000 customers upgrade their workspaces. We're still bootstrapped, still obsessed with quality, and still charging fair prices.
